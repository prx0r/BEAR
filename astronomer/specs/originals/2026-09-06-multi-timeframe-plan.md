## Final Notes

Notice how recently, I was macro spot positioned, positional long, swing short and scalp long, yet all of the trades managed to co-exist and work out and were in alignment with the overall multi-timeframe plan.

That's because the timeframes from macro -> daily/positional -> swing -> scalp are big enough jumps (factor of 12 or similar) to not affect each other.

They also allow to affect each other through bias -> execution when stepping up or down one timeframe.

That brings the beauty of multi timeframe positioning full circle, helps you stay sharp and when the market does something unexpected, still allows you to profit most of the time.

It's imo a myth to align your "personality" with your trading timeframe. Not investing just because you don't have the patience seems like a detrimental issue, or not taking a swing trade while one of the best swing trade opportunities arises, equally.

So with all that said, I disclosed roughly all positions I hold, why and so you know on all timeframes where I expect price to go + actions towards it.
